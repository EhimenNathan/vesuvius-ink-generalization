## MAN5 alignment — completed, exploratory

Sources: completed main-account `vesuvius-ink-h2hfix-b0p256` and
`vesuvius-ink9um-fixed`; labels from `vesuvius-ink-prep3`.
All six maps use the same 150,000-pixel sample (seed 42), drawn from the intersection
of map coverage and supervision eroded by eight prepared pixels. Offsets are
-8..8 in steps of 2, with `prediction(y,x)` scored against `label(y+dy,x+dx)`.

Interpretation: no large, coherent interior optimum establishes misregistration
as the dominant B0 failure. Boundary optima mean the search does not identify a

scores because the fixed diagnostic population and uint8 maps are used. Do not
apply the selected shifts to headline test scores. Spatially adjacent pixels are
correlated; this sample is not 150,000 independent observations.

## MAN5 depth and scale — depth sampled; physical spacing unresolved

The source L0 shape is [65, 24120, 29900]. Three supervised-neighborhood chunk
profiles (seed 42) peak at pooled indices 7, 10 and 6 of 17. This argues against
grossly absent papyrus in these sampled chunks; intensity peaks do not identify
the ink depth or prove an optimal window. No test-label-selected window is used.

The source `.zattrs` names micrometer units but gives L0 [1,1,1] and pyramid
factors [2,2,2], [4,4,4]. The tifxyz `meta.json` lacks a scan reference. Therefore
the physical L0 voxel size is **not established**. The prepared `um=9.6` value
is assigned by the builder, not measured evidence. Do not treat MAN5 as verified
at the correct physical scale until the upstream scan/render metadata is found.

## Paris4 smoothing — completed

The downloaded seed43 B0 result explicitly holds Paris4 out of training. It
contains eight segments: w00, w01, w02, w03, w05, w06, w07, w09. This differs from
the handoff's seven-segment label. The diagnostic tries sigma 0, .5, 1, 2, 3
prepared pixels, selects equal-segment mean AUC, and excludes a fixed 12-pixel
crop margin for every sigma. Paris4 is a development fold, not fresh test evidence.

| Sigma, prepared pixels | Mean segment AUC |
|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0.6918129115 |
| 0.5 | 0.6919275240 |
| 1 | 0.6919392335 |
| 2 | 0.6919262302 |
| 3 | 0.6918738272 |

Freeze sigma=1 as secondary post-processing. The gain is only 0.0001263221;
there is no evidence here of a material generalization improvement. Unsmoothed
output remains primary. The future final models should report both, without
reselecting sigma on the four test sources.

## Fresh pixel proof and recovered control records

`verify_pyramid_pixels.py` independently compared public 0841 L0 chunk `0.60.72`
and L2 chunk `0.15.18`, using an aligned 32x32 XY region selected from dimensions
without labels. Across all 17 L2 slices, correlation with XY-only-pooled L0 slice
at the same depth index is 0.999885–0.999918, and mean absolute error is
0.321–0.344 uint8 intensity units. The alternative four-slice depth-pooling
hypothesis has larger error. This confirms slice truncation for this region.
Four-slice pooling here is a hypothesis test for how L2 was built, **not** the
physical-scale repair: 0841 requires factor two at verified 4.681 um input.

Recovered completed-run result JSON confirms the 1667 pooled reverse controls:

| Released checkpoint | Published L2 | Rebuilt input |
|---|---:|---:|
| Seed42 75k | 0.5467485800 | 0.9112196127 |
| Seed43 75k | 0.5360722770 | 0.9340863470 |

These are trained-on pipeline controls, not evidence of unseen-scroll
generalization. The inference was not rerun in this CPU audit. Sampling and
label identity across before/after still need a coordinate-level provenance
check before describing the comparison as strictly identical pixels.

`release/vc-multiscan/pool_surface.py` now provides chunked pooling from an
explicit, externally verified voxel size, with chosen factors and actual output
spacing recorded. It refuses overwrite and incompatible physical scales and
correctly averages partial boundary blocks. Factor selection and edge means
passed synthetic tests. It has not rebuilt a full real scroll in this audit.

## Claims and GPU priorities

- The 33-entry local pyramid survey has 18 metadata-inconsistency flags and 15
  unflagged entries. Pixel-level proof and control recovery are separate evidence.
  The survey flags alone do not prove truncation in each of the 18 volumes.
- Historical pooled ensemble scores include mis-scaled 0841 and cannot support
  current superiority claims. Score corrected data on identical pixels first.
- The actual Saturday job file specifies B0 seed44, 30k steps. The older
  submission plan's DINOv3 schedule is superseded by that file.
- B0 seeds42/43 in the handoff are 12k runs, not 30k runs. Training steps, batch
  sizes, patches, seeds, parameter counts and wall time must all be reported.
- Focal+Dice does not establish calibration. A fixed .5 threshold is a policy,
  not a calibration result. Rank scores are percentiles, not ink probabilities.
- EMA remains primary; SWA is secondary. Ensemble membership, normalization,
  direction rule and post-processing need frozen declarations before new runs.
- Existing four-source results have already informed development; describe them
  as training-source-held-out evaluation, not untouched final-test evidence.
- Stronger generalization evidence needs source-level folds, source-level metrics,
  controls and another untouched source where feasible. Mixed-render training
  must exclude all renders of each held-out source, not merely one file format.
- Keep external ink_9um ensembles secondary and credit Andreas Hadjigeorgiou and
  Paulo Camillo. Do not claim novel depth-order dependence.
